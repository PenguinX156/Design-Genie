# Contributing

Keep changes tied to an observed design or usability problem. Add only source-verified resource metadata, including an official URL, license evidence, and usage classification. Unknown licenses remain link-only. Do not bundle proprietary screenshots or components.

Run `npm test` after changing the scanner or CLI. For visual workflow changes, exercise the Campaign Lab brief at desktop and mobile, record what changed in the critique, and compare against the baseline without tuning the rubric to favor the treatment. Keep portable skills short and validate their frontmatter.
